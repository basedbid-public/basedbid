# basedbid product demo

[Watch the final Colosseum demo](https://youtu.be/xJ4ocTRwGzg)

Launching on basedbid is simple, even with advanced features.

Our full-release demo follows a **Pro Flash Token launch** using the **Fee Builder** and **Rewards Basket**:

1. Enter the token details and upload a logo.
2. Choose the network, supported DEX and quote asset.
3. Configure where trading fees go and how much each route receives.
4. Select supported reward assets and set their allocations.
5. Review the settings, confirm in the wallet and launch.

Trading fees fund the configured rewards without selling the launched token. Fee routes and basket settings can change after launch without redeploying.

*Available DEXs, assets and settings vary by network. basedbid supports Solana, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Arc and Robinhood Chain.*

## Release status

At submission, [based.bid](https://www.based.bid/) hosts the existing mainnet beta. The demo shows the updated full release, which is still in final testing and is not yet publicly accessible. It will replace the beta on the same domain once ready. Updated contracts may be redeployed before release.

The Fee Builder and EVM Rewards Basket predate the competition. Competition-period work comprises the Solana Rewards Basket, redesigned Solana Fee Builder and Pre-Launch. This demo follows a Flash Token launch, not all three launch types.
