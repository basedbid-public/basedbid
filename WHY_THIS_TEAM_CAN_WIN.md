## We know the customer

Our work at GemPad put us directly alongside projects raising funds, launching tokens and managing their communities. We saw where creators needed better support, where launch settings became restrictive and how much work went into maintaining the underlying infrastructure.

That experience shaped basedbid. Creators need economics they can adjust after launch. Partners need launch technology they can offer under their own brand without rebuilding the contracts and backend.

Our Fee Builder and Rewards Baskets address the first need. Boards and our integration tools address the second.

## How we plan to win

We serve both projects launching tokens and platforms offering launches to their own users.

That gives us two routes to adoption: our own interface and partner products built on the same infrastructure. basedbid earns a share of trading fees from both, so the business can grow through ongoing trading activity as well as new launches.

Our advantage is the combination of launch experience, working multichain technology and existing partner demand. We've already built the product and supported real usage. The next step is making it easier for more creators and platforms to use it.

**Our goal is to make basedbid the default choice for launching a token or building a launchpad.**
