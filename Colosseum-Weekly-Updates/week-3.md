# Week 3 | Launch interface and Fee Builder walkthrough

[Watch the Week 3 update](https://youtu.be/hC_3lqsbESg)

## Features shown

This one-minute product video shows the updated launch interface and fee configuration:

- Market discovery and token previews.
- Token details and network selection.
- Starting market-cap and supply settings.
- Fee Builder strategies, including creator revenue, buybacks and basket rewards.
- Custom receiving-wallet routes and allocations.
- Adding reward assets by address where supported.
- Automatic distribution settings for collected fees across enabled routes.

The emphasis is on bringing launch configuration and programmable fee routing into one interface.

## Development and release context

The Fee Builder and EVM Rewards Basket existed before the competition. The Solana Rewards Basket, redesigned Solana Fee Builder and Pre-Launch are the confirmed competition-period developments.

This walkthrough shows configuration, not a completed launch transaction or holder payout.
