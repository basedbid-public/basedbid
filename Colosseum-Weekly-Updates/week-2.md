# Week 2 | Rewards Baskets on Solana

[Watch the Week 2 update](https://youtu.be/X4p9POLiGNg)

## Progress shown

This update takes a closer look at the Solana Rewards Basket, extending capabilities that already existed on EVM networks.

The preview demonstrates:

- Presets and custom asset baskets.
- Supported tokenized stocks, stablecoins and other tokens.
- Adding or removing assets and adjusting their weights.
- Equal allocations.
- Minimum holding requirements.
- Combined or rotating payouts.
- Automatic distribution once the configured threshold is reached.

## Development context

The EVM Rewards Basket predates the competition. Building the Solana Rewards Basket is part of the current competition-period work.

The video shows release functionality in development, not proof of completed holder payouts.
